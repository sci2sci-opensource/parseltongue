# Governance Policy — Amendment 2025-A

Issued by the Data Governance Board. This amendment supplements the
Enterprise Data Governance Policy; where it imposes stricter duties, the
stricter duty prevails. No data changes are required for this amendment
to take effect — compliance is re-evaluated against the estate as it is.

## A1. Scheduled Audit Cadence

Contracts covering restricted data must provide for scheduled audits at least annually; quarterly reviews satisfy this requirement. Bi-annual and on-demand audit arrangements do not satisfy the scheduled-cadence requirement.

## A2. De-identification Carve-out

Where a contract requires de-identification of the covered data, the datasets it covers may cross geographic regions without a separate transfer agreement.

## A3. Cross-Border Transfer Agreements

Restricted data covered by a geographically restricted contract may not be distributed across regions unless a transfer agreement is documented in the contract. A geographically restricted contract without a documented transfer agreement is non-compliant, except where the de-identification carve-out applies.

## Effect on Compliance Status

An estate is amendment-consistent when it is policy-consistent under the base policy and additionally satisfies the scheduled audit cadence and cross-border transfer requirements of this amendment.
