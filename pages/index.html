parseltongue demos

Analysis of a fictional AI2AI Series A investor memo. Each notebook pattern extracts facts from the memo, derives financial metrics, validates business rules, and renders a grounded narrative where every claim links back to source evidence.

  • Standalone
    Everything in one file — facts, computation, and prose. Self-contained analysis of ARR, margins, and unit economics.
  • Explicit
    Imports a shared facts module, derives metrics inline. Shows how notebooks connect to larger pltg systems.
  • Implicit
    Pure narrative — imports pre-computed facts and rules. Clean prose with grounded references, no visible computation.
  • Markdown edge cases
    Stress test of the renderer — headings, tables, nested lists, blockquotes, inline formatting, and adjacent refs.
